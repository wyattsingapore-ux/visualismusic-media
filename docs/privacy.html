Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-17

VisualisMusic ("we", "us") produces guitar performance guide videos published to our own accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

What we collect

Our publishing tools use official platform APIs (TikTok Login Kit / Content Posting API, Meta Graph API) solely to authenticate our own accounts and upload video content we created. We do not collect, store, or process personal data belonging to viewers or other platform users.

Access tokens

OAuth access and refresh tokens issued to us are stored locally and used only to publish our own content. They are never shared with third parties.

Third-party platforms

Each platform (TikTok, Meta, YouTube) has its own privacy policy governing data collected when you view content there.
